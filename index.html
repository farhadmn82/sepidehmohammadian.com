About

Sepideh Mohammaidan was born in 1986 in Tehran, Iran. Sepideh Mohammadian is an author, screenwriter and researcher. She got her BA in Law and her MA in sociology, fluent in Farsi, English, French and Turkish. She has been writing for 18 years now, working with Roshangaran Publishings since the begining of her career as a writer. She has worked with several literary magazines in Iran having published short stories.

She works on semiotics in films and the main focus in her researches are social, racial and sexual minorities. She has written 7 books some of which are banned in Iran.

After taking a screen writing course with Naser Taghvae, she collaborated with other artists on scripts. She also wrote a play that went on stage at Vahdat Hall in 2014.
